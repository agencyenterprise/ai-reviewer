Comments on "Curbside Organics Collection: What Twelve Municipal Programs
Reveal About Public Acceptance"

This is a useful contribution and I recommend publication after revision. I
have three substantive concerns and a number of small corrections.

Substantive

First, the outcome coding does most of the analytical work and I do not think
it holds up. A program is "abandoned" if it was defunded for more than 36
months, but three of the five abandoned programs were in states whose landfill
diversion mandate was repealed partway through the study period. Abandoning a
mandate response after the mandate lapses is not the same kind of failure as
abandoning one under public pressure, and the report treats them identically.
I would either recode or add a sensitivity analysis that drops those cases.

Second, Finding 5 claims a single elected opponent was "decisive" in four of
five abandoned programs. Decisive is a causal word and the design cannot
support it. The report has twelve cases, no counterfactual, and an outcome
that co-occurs with opposition. I would rewrite this as an association and
say plainly what the design can and cannot establish.

Third, the report never addresses selection into the sample. Twelve programs
were compiled from city records and press coverage, which means programs that
were killed early and quietly are systematically less likely to appear. If the
missing cases are disproportionately failures without a vocal opponent,
Finding 5 is partly an artifact. Please discuss.

Smaller points

- Page 1, "The operations are well understood" is too strong. Say the
  operations are mature and well characterized.
- Finding 2 refers to Table 2, but the report contains no Table 2.
- Finding 4 refers to Figure 3; I count only two figures.
- The interview count is given as 23 staff, but the Method section does not say
  how many municipalities those 23 came from.
- "Coded objections" in Finding 3 needs a sentence on who did the coding and
  whether agreement was measured.
- The 14 percent figure in Finding 3 has no denominator. Fourteen percent of
  how many objections?
- Reference 3 (Reyes 2017) is cited nowhere in the text.
- The date range is given as 2008 to 2023 in the About This section and as
  2008 to 2022 in the Method section.
- Please expand the term "organics" at first use in the Findings; it is defined
  in the About This section but the Findings read as a standalone section.
