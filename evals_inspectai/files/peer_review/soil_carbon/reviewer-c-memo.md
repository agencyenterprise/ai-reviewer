Reviewer C comments

- The piece needs a proper methods section describing how the literature was
  searched and which studies were included.

- I would like to see a meta-analysis of the reported sequestration rates
  across the long-term trials the author refers to, with a forest plot.

- Please add a table comparing the major soil carbon protocols on measurement
  requirements, permanence terms, and additionality tests.

- The claims about no-till adoption trends should be supported with a figure
  showing adoption over the thirty-year period.

- The author should state a position on whether voluntary market credits should
  also be restricted, and support it with the same three-part analysis.

- There are no references at all. At minimum the empirical claims about
  variation, tillage losses, and adoption trends need citations.
